02Design principles

Identity through proportion and contrast. Color belongs to the content.

01 / CANVAS FIRST

Give the image the stage.

Keep controls at the perimeter. Preserve aspect ratio. The separate output contains only the artwork.

02 / INSTRUMENT LOGIC

Make the signal readable.

Source → effects → output. Keep selection, bypass and failures explicit. Show details where the user is working.

03 / RESTRAINED ENERGY

Use one accent with intent.

Accent means action, selection or focus. Neutral surfaces stay independent of scene colors. Respect the Mac accent.

03Foundations

Semantic colors adapt to light, dark and increased contrast. All dimensions use the existing 4pt spacing family.
